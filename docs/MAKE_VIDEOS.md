# Make Reddit story videos (inside the website)

Everything happens on the website, in **Chrome or Edge on a computer**. Nothing to install.

## 1. Find stories (📖 Stories page)
1. Click **📥 Find stories on Reddit**. Drag the **📥 Shorts HQ Finder** button to your bookmarks bar (once).
2. Open **reddit.com** and make sure you're logged in.
3. Click the **📥 Shorts HQ Finder** bookmark. A Shorts HQ window opens with the top stories from the best
   subreddits (AITA, pettyrevenge, MaliciousCompliance, tifu…), each rated ⭐ with its video length.
4. Tick the ones you like → **Save ticked stories**. Your friend sees the same board.

Why a bookmark? Since 2026 Reddit blocks apps from reading stories automatically, but it still works
when *you* are logged in on reddit.com. On a single Reddit post, the bookmark grabs just that story.
You can also **Add one by hand** (paste the link, title and text).

## 2. Ask the author
Stories belong to the people who wrote them. On each story click **✉️ Ask the author** (a ready-made
polite message opens on Reddit). When they say yes, click **✅ Author said yes**.
Click **🙋 I'll do it** so your friend knows you're making that one.

## 3. Make the video (🎬 Make video)
1. Check the text (swear words are replaced automatically — better for YouTube money). Cut boring parts if you like.
2. Pick a voice and speed (**▶ Test the voice**). The first time the free AI voice downloads (~90 MB).
3. **Add a background video** once — a long gameplay video like 2 hours of Minecraft parkour (MP4).
   Every video uses a different random part of it, exactly as long as the story. The site remembers which
   minutes were used. The big file stays on your computer (each person adds their own copy).
4. Click **🎬 Make the video**. Keep the tab open; it takes about as long as the video.
   Stories longer than ~2½ minutes become **Part 1, Part 2…** automatically (Shorts max is 3 minutes).
5. Watch it, then **📅 Add to queue** (or **⬇ Download**). It posts at the Reddit Stories page's times.

The video has: the Reddit post card while the title is read → big word-by-word captions (current word in
yellow) → the voice → your background. 1080×1920, 30 fps, MP4.

## Make many at once (batch)
On the Stories board, tick several stories (or **Tick all allowed**) → **🎬 Make selected**. Pick the voice,
background and page once, then **Make all**. Each story becomes its own video (long ones get parts) and goes
straight into the queue. Stories whose author hasn't said yes are skipped (or tick "The author said yes" next to them).

## Approve before posting
When one of you makes or uploads a video, it shows **⏳ Waiting for approval** on the Queue page. The other person
taps **▶** to watch it, then **✅ Approve** or **✏️ Send back** with a note. Only approved videos post.
Turn this off in Settings → General if you don't want it.

## Calendar
**🗓 Calendar** shows what posts when for both pages (2 or 4 weeks). Drag a video to another day — or tap it — to give
it a fixed date and time. Empty posting times are shown, so you know when to make more.
